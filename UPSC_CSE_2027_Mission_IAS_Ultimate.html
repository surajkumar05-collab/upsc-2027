📅 Master Roadmap

आज 30 Sep 2026 से realistic phase-wise plan.

Phase 1 · 01 Oct–31 Dec 2026 — Foundation
NCERT + standard books, newspaper/current affairs system, basic CSAT, optional का 60–70%, रोज़ 20–30 PYQ/MCQ, weekly answer writing.
Phase 2 · 01 Jan–28 Feb 2027 — Integrated GS
GS-I to IV first cycle, optional first cycle complete, PYQ 2013–26, CSAT weekly, essay हर सप्ताह, monthly revision.
Phase 3 · 01 Mar–30 Apr 2027 — Prelims Intensive
Static revision + current affairs + 2–3 full mocks/week; CSAT 2 sessions/week; error notebook; elimination practice.
Phase 4 · 01–22 May 2027 — Final Prelims Sprint
नई किताब/नया source नहीं. Multiple revisions, PYQ, maps, facts, test analysis, sleep and exam logistics.
Phase 5 · 24 May–19 Aug 2027 — Mains Mode
GS answer writing daily, Essay weekly, Optional intensive, Ethics case studies, sectional/full tests, value addition.
Phase 6 · 20 Aug onward — Personality Test
DAF, current affairs, home state/district, education, hobbies, service preference, mock interviews.
